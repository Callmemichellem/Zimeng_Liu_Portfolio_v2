Nov 2023 — Mar 2024
Research Intern · Coal & Power
Shanxi Securities Research Institute | 山西证券研究所煤炭与电力组Beijing, China
  • Used Excel and Wind to clean and compare roughly five years of coal-industry and mine-accident data for sector monitoring and research.
  • Developed an Indonesia coal-industry study integrating government production/export statistics, company disclosures, realised selling prices, cash-cost normalisation and policy evidence.
  • Found that 2023 Indonesian coal output reached 775.2 Mt (+12.8% YoY) and exports 518 Mt (+11.4%), yet representative producers still saw EBITDA declines of roughly 43–49%.
  • Built a price-sensitivity model in which a 10% selling-price decline reduced modelled cash contribution by about 29.8%, reinforcing the conclusion that price exposure and unit economics matter more than volume growth alone.

Jul 2023 — Aug 2023
Data Processing Intern
Environmental Defense Fund (EDF) | 美国环保协会Beijing, China
  • Cleaned and validated multi-source environmental datasets for Yellow River governance research and prepared visual analytical outputs linking ecological indicators with policy and regional context.
  • The broader research dataset showed 2023 good-quality river sections at 91.0%, agriculture accounting for 64.6% of basin water use, and 251,100 km² still affected by soil erosion.
  • A scenario analysis showed that a 5% reduction in agricultural gross-water use equated to 1.267 billion m³ of gross savings before conversion to net water savings.
