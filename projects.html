RAIDS Lab · Research

GraphRAG for Evidence-Based ESG Analysis

Sydney, Australia · The University of Sydney

Problem. When does graph-structured retrieval materially outperform standard RAG on real corporate ESG reports?

Work. Built a shared ESG corpus and evaluation benchmark; compared DualChannel with HippoRAG v1/v2, LightRAG and HyperGraphRAG under controlled settings.

30corporate ESG reports
2,015benchmark questions
4graph methods compared

Finding. GraphRAG was task-dependent rather than universally superior: HyperGraphRAG was strongest for complex reasoning/evidence-based generation, LightRAG for summarisation coverage, while lower-cost retrieval remained preferable for simpler questions.

Corporate Finance · Team Project

TPG Telecom Capital Structure & Credit Analysis

Sydney, Australia · University of Sydney
Company context. TPG Telecom is a Top-100 ASX-listed company and describes itself as the second-largest ASX-listed telecommunications company in Australia; ACCC data placed the group at 17% of retail mobile services at June 2025.

Problem. How should TPG deploy expected proceeds from the Vocus infrastructure transaction to strengthen credit quality while preserving shareholder value?

Method. Compared 4 telecom companies across 6 financial metrics, including debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, liquidity and valuation; reviewed debt maturity structure and Moody's leverage thresholds.

3.23×TPG debt / EBITDA
5.24×EBITDA net interest coverage
A$5.25bnexpected sale proceeds in project case

Recommendation. Allocate 80% of proceeds to debt reduction and 20% to a special dividend, prioritising credit improvement over buybacks given relative valuation.

Financial Crime & Regulation · Team Research

Australia's Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Regime

Sydney, Australia · University of Sydney · CLAW6031

Compared FATF Recommendations 24 and 25 with Australia's proposed beneficial-ownership reforms and overseas models including the UK and Netherlands.

The research identified Australia's then-current Partially Compliant status on Recommendation 24 and Non-Compliant status on Recommendation 25, and argued that phased implementation plus weak government-led verification could limit effectiveness.
