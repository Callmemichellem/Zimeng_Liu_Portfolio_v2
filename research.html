Own research · experiment design

Ethnic Cues in AI Hiring

Do names and accents change how large language models screen resumes and score AI interviews? The study audits both AI stages that now sit before a human interviewer, with Chinese applicants as the main case.

2AI hiring stages studied
1,176names in the name bank (built)
12Australian roles planned
~30,600planned Study 1 calls per model

Design: Study 1 has models from 8 Chinese and US developers score identical resumes that differ only in name origin, surname spelling, work rights or employer type. Study 2 has the same interview answer read in different accents with different names, scored by transcribe-then-score and direct speech pipelines, plus text versions that separate Chinese-English word order from ordinary errors.

Status: The name bank, scoring and analysis scripts and literature base are built; the pilot and pre-registration come next. Hypotheses are two-sided with ±2-point equivalence tests, so “no difference” is a valid result.

Assisting · RAIDS Lab · University of Sydney

GraphRAG for Evidence-Based ESG Analysis

Lingjing Lan's experiment compares a production non-graph retriever with four GraphRAG systems on real corporate sustainability reports. I assist with report annotation, benchmark construction and retrieval comparisons.

30corporate reports (about 1,974 pages)
2,015questions across four task types
+15–23points of fact-retrieval accuracy for graph methods
5–6×context tokens for LightRAG and HyperGraphRAG

Methods: PaddleOCR layout parsing and rule-based table statements; the non-graph DualChannel baseline reuses the BM25 + vector retrieval of the EulerESG production system and is compared with HippoRAG, HippoRAG 2, LightRAG and HyperGraphRAG under the same answer model, prompts and scoring.

Main findings: Graph methods clearly help fact retrieval, but no single method wins every task: HyperGraphRAG leads on complex reasoning, LightRAG on summary coverage, and HippoRAG 2 offers the best balance of quality and cost. The team's current focus is indicator identity: evidence must match the same disclosure code, year, unit and scope.

My role: Assisting Lingjing Lan with her experiment. Supervised by Dr. Zhengyi Yang.
